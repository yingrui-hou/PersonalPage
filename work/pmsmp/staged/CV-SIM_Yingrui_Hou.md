## PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE

### Postdoctoral Researcher | CNRS - LPCA, Clermont-Ferrand | 2023-2025

### GRAiNITA | Physics-based simulation and instrumented prototype validation

- Modelled the response of a scintillating-grain prototype by linking June 2024 CERN SPS H9 measurements with Geant4 performance simulations.

- Extracted response-uniformity maps from a 16-channel prototype using track reconstruction, channel corrections and 2D spatial analysis.

- Integrated measured maps into a simplified full-size module simulation to estimate the impact of non-uniformity on resolution.

- Evaluated the resolution budget: photo-electron statistics near 1%/sqrt(E) and a non-uniformity constant term estimated below 1% in the studied configuration, with E in GeV.

- Contributed to experimental validation using WLS fibres, SiPMs and DWC beam tracking to connect measured response with simulation assumptions.

### PSD studies | Time-resolved signals and energy reconstruction

- Developed ZnWO4/BGO scintillation simulations from 1 to 20 GeV with particle-dependent time constants to assess signal-separation capabilities.

- Extracted time-profile components through fitting and applied a correlation-based correction, improving resolution by about a factor of 2 in a representative simulation study.

- Compared material timing signatures and identified insufficient separation in BGO to guide the interpretation of PSD studies.
