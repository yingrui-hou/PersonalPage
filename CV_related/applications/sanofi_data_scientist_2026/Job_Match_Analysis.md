# Sanofi Data Scientist - Match Analysis

Job: Data Scientist, Manufacturing & Supply, Lyon  
Contract: Permanent, full time; hybrid 60% office / 40% remote  
Posting date: 19 August 2026  
Closing date: 26 August 2026

## Overall Assessment

Estimated evidence-based match: **58-65%**.

This is a credible stretch application. The profile matches the quantitative education requirement and much of the modelling, validation, monitoring, complex-data, HPC/Linux, and cross-functional scientific work. The largest gaps are not academic credentials; they are industrialisation signals. The posting expects a data scientist who has delivered production-ready AI/ML products, worked with cloud and MLOps stacks, used enterprise databases and APIs, and can mentor junior staff.

## Strong Matches

- PhD in physics with strong Python/C++ coding experience.
- Statistical inference, probabilistic modelling, maximum-likelihood estimation, calibration, and uncertainty analysis.
- CatBoost / gradient-boosting workflows, supervised learning, feature validation, train/test diagnostics, and dataset-shift mitigation.
- Time-dependent modelling under noise, monitoring, anomaly diagnosis, and root-cause analysis.
- Large and complex scientific datasets, Linux/HPC-style environments, reproducible workflows, Git, CI/CD, and shared codebases.
- Model robustness and decision-facing validation in technically critical measurement environments.
- International collaboration, technical review, scientific communication, initiative, and problem solving.

## Partial Matches

- **Forecasting:** strong time-dependent inference and noisy time-series modelling, but no documented industrial forecasting project.
- **Optimisation:** numerical performance optimisation is documented, but not operations research or constrained business optimisation.
- **Data visualisation/storytelling:** matplotlib and seaborn are documented; no Tableau, Power BI, Streamlit, or Plotly evidence.
- **Large-scale data:** substantial collaboration-scale data processing is documented, but not Spark/Databricks-style distributed data engineering.
- **Responsible AI:** robust validation, uncertainty, monitoring, and bias awareness transfer well, but no documented AI-governance or regulatory implementation.
- **Leadership:** collaboration and reusable component contributions are documented, but formal mentoring of junior data scientists is not.

## Missing or Weakly Evidenced Requirements

- Production model deployment and product-focused agile delivery.
- Cloud platforms such as AWS or GCP; Databricks and Apache Spark.
- SQL, NoSQL, graph, and time-series databases.
- MLOps lifecycle ownership, orchestration, model serving, and production monitoring.
- Enterprise API design or integration.
- Deep learning, LLMs, reinforcement learning, and federated learning.
- Pharmaceutical/healthcare manufacturing and sensitive healthcare-data experience.
- AI regulations and formal responsible-AI governance.
- Formal mentoring or people leadership.

## Short-Term Gap Plan

### Feasible in 2-4 weeks

- Build one manufacturing-style time-series project using a public sensor dataset: forecasting, anomaly detection, feature pipeline, validation by time split, and a small Streamlit or Plotly interface.
- Learn practical SQL through PostgreSQL or DuckDB and expose the project data through a documented schema and reproducible queries.
- Package a model behind a FastAPI endpoint with tests, Docker, model metadata, and basic drift/performance monitoring.
- Learn the essentials of GxP data integrity, the EU AI Act, model cards, audit trails, and responsible-AI documentation.

### Feasible in 1-3 months

- Deploy the project on one cloud platform and add CI/CD, experiment tracking, orchestration, and monitoring.
- Complete a focused Spark/Databricks course and reproduce part of the pipeline with distributed processing.
- Add forecasting baselines and one constrained optimisation example tied to manufacturing decisions.

### Not credibly fixable by coursework alone

- Real production ownership in a regulated pharmaceutical environment.
- Enterprise-scale MLOps delivery and formal mentoring experience.

These should be acknowledged honestly and offset with strong evidence of rapid domain learning, rigorous validation, and collaborative software delivery.
